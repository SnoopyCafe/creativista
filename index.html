FAQ

Questions families ask before joining a pod.

What is a learning pod?

A small group of children working together to learn, explore, socialize and have fun, supervised by a teacher, tutor, or guide. Families gather their child's friends, neighbors or classmates, and we provide the teacher.

How many students are in a learning pod?

We suggest up to six children. A pod can be as small as three. We can help you form your own pod, or your child can join an existing one.

What is required to host a learning pod at your home?

A space in your home: a play area, den, Florida room, living room, dining room, backyard. Essentially a table and five chairs. We visit first to agree on the space. We supply all the materials, art supplies and games.

What are the qualifications of the teachers?

Experience in the field of Education, with degrees in Early Childhood, Elementary, or ESE. All teachers are insured, fingerprinted, and background checked.

What curriculum is used?

For the youngest pods, play-based learning is used. For older pods, the curriculum is provided by families and/or teacher-generated.

How long does each pod last?

Term 1: September 1 to December 18 (No sessions during Thanksgiving week or the last two weeks in December)

Term 2: January 4 to May 28th (No sessions during one week in March)

Where do families live?

Families live in Broward, Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade Counties.

Do you provide pods for homeschooled children only?

No, after school pods are available in addition to homeschool pods.
